10. Phong lighting

Phong lighting adds three kinds of light: ambient (everywhere), diffuse (facing the light) and specular (the shiny highlight). Turn each one on and off.

  • The light (small sphere)

What to notice

  • Diffuse only: the side facing the light is bright, the far side is black. That's dot(normal, toLight).
  • Specular moves when the camera moves; diffuse doesn't. The highlight depends on where your eye is.
  • Shininess changes the highlight's size, not its brightness. Low = plastic, high = polished metal.

The code

// Fragment shader (engine/3d/lighting.js), simplified:
float diffuse  = max(dot(normal, toLight), 0.0);
float specular = pow(max(dot(reflect(-toLight, normal), toCamera), 0.0), shininess);
color = base * light * (ambient + diffuse) + light * specular * specularStrength;
