Playtest and ship

Game design track · lesson 3 of 3 · about 25 minutes

You cannot see your own game fresh. Someone else can, in five minutes.

1 The idea

A playtest is watching another person play while you stay quiet. You will want to explain; don't. Every time you would have explained something, write it down: that is a problem the game must solve on its own, because the real players will be alone.

Watch for: where they hesitate, what they try that does not work, when they smile, and when they stop. Then fix the biggest problem only, and test again. Two short playtests beat one long one.

Shipping is part of the game. A game that is not uploaded does not exist for the jam. Upload a working build early, then replace it.

2 See it

Practice on this: ask someone to play Pong for two minutes on your computer, with no instructions. Watch and write.

Open it in its own tab (easier on a phone).

3 Walkthrough

  1. Set up: open the game, hand over the keyboard, and say only: "Play as if I'm not here. Think out loud if you like."
  2. Watch, write, do not help. Use a timeline: 0:10 pressed Space, nothing happened, 0:40 laughed at the bounce, 1:30 didn't notice the score.
  3. Ask three questions after: What was the goal? What was confusing? What was the best moment?
  4. Fix one thing: the biggest confusion, usually controls or goal. Then test with someone new.
  5. Ship: zip the game folder's contents (with index.html at the top level), upload as an HTML game on itch.io, set the viewport size, test the uploaded page, and check the jam's rules, including whether it asks you to disclose AI help. Every step is in SUBMIT-TO-ITCH.md.

4 Exercise

Playtest one Kindling sample (or your own game) with one person for five minutes. Fill in the template below, then name the one change you would make.

Game:            Tester:            Date:
Timeline (time: what happened):
What was the goal? (their words)
What was confusing?
Best moment?
The ONE change I will make:
Hint 1

If they are stuck for more than 30 seconds, you may say one sentence. Write down that you had to.

Hint 2

"Make it more fun" is not a change. "Show the controls on the title screen" is.

Solution

An example of a filled-in sheet:

Game: Pong sample        Tester: my brother        Date: Saturday
Timeline:
  0:00  looked for a start button, clicked the screen (that works: mouse:0 starts)
  0:15  used arrow keys straight away
  0:50  "oh, the edges send it sideways!"   <- best moment
  1:40  lost 3-7, asked "can I play against you?"
What was the goal?   "get 7 points first"
What was confusing?  "nothing really, the computer is too good at the start"
Best moment?         hitting with the paddle edge
The ONE change:      COMPUTER_SPEED starts at 80 and rises with each point
