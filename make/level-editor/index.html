Level editor

Paint a level, press Play to try it, then copy it into your game. It saves itself in this browser as you work.

      Arrows or A/D to move, Space or W to jump, Esc to stop.

      Size

      Open a level

      Paste a level

      Paste rows of text, a JavaScript array like ["####", "#..#"], or a whole level.js.

      Use it in your game

      Copy this into level.js (or download it), then load it with createTilemap({ rows: LEVEL, solid: "#B", oneWay: "=" }). In the platformer sample it replaces one entry of LEVELS in samples/2d/04-platformer/level.js.

      Tips

      • Keys: B paint, R rectangle, G fill, I pick, S scroll, 1–9 choose a tile, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo.
      • Right-click (or the Empty tile) erases. On a phone, switch to Scroll to move around a big level with your finger.
      • Rectangle with Shift held draws only the outline: quick walls and rooms.
      • Play-test often. A jump that looks fine on the grid can be one tile too far.
