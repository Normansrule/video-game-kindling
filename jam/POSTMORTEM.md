# Postmortem template

Write it within two days of the jam, while you still remember. Copy this into your game's repository as `POSTMORTEM.md`, or post it on the jam page: other developers read these.

```markdown
# Postmortem: GAME NAME (JAM NAME, THEME)

Link: ...
Time spent: ... hours of ... · Solo / team of ...

## The pitch
One sentence: You VERB to GOAL, but PROBLEM.

## What went right (3 things)
1.
2.
3.

## What went wrong (3 things)
1.
2.
3.

## The plan versus what happened
| Feature | Planned (must/should/could) | Guessed hours | Real hours | Shipped? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## What players said
Quote the most common comment, the nicest one, and the most useful criticism.

## Kindling notes
What did the engine make easy? What was missing or confusing? (Open an issue or a
discussion on the Kindling repository so the next jam goes better.)

## Next time I will
1.
2.
```

## Questions that help

- Where did the first quarter of the jam go? Did the core verb exist by then?
- Which estimate was furthest off, and why?
- What did playtesters do that surprised you?
- What would you cut if you did it again?
